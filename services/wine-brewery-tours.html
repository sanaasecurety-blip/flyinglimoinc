How Many Tastings Fit in a Day

Three tastings is the sweet spot for most groups — enough to make the trip feel full without palate fatigue setting in. We build in at least 90 minutes per stop, including drive time, and always plan the first appointment for late morning when tasting rooms tend to run more relaxed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many wineries can we visit in a day?

Three tastings is the sweet spot for most groups, with time for lunch. We can plan two for an unhurried day or up to four for a fuller itinerary.

Do you help book winery reservations?

We can route your day around reservations you've already made, and offer recommendations based on your preferences — reservations themselves are made directly with the winery.

What vehicles are available for wine tours?

Sedans and SUVs work well for couples or small groups, while our Sprinter van is popular for larger parties touring together.
