Wedding & Special Event Transportation

Weddings · Proposals · Anniversaries · Bay Area Wide

Transportation is one of the details guests notice most when it goes wrong — and one couples rarely have to think about when it's handled well. Flying Limo covers the wedding party, the couple, and out-of-town guests with the same punctuality as every other service we offer.

What's Included

Sprinter vans for full wedding parties, photo-ready

A private sedan or SUV for the couple

Guest shuttles on a loop for hotel blocks

Buffer built into every leg for hair, makeup, and photos

Custom requests: music, decor space, dress accommodation

A day-of coordinator contact for your driver or dispatcher

Planned Around Your Timeline

The best chauffeurs treat your printed wedding-day timeline as a starting point, not a fixed script — adjusting in real time if hair and makeup runs ahead or the ceremony starts late. That flexibility is the difference between transportation that supports your day and transportation you have to manage yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should we book wedding transportation?

We recommend booking at least one to two months ahead, especially for peak wedding season weekends, so your preferred vehicles are guaranteed.

How many vehicles do we need for our wedding party?

It depends on your party size, but a Sprinter van typically covers a full wedding party in one vehicle, while a separate sedan or SUV is common for the couple.

Can you accommodate a guest shuttle for our hotel block?

Yes. We run guest shuttles on a set loop between hotel blocks and venues so guests aren't left arranging their own rides.
