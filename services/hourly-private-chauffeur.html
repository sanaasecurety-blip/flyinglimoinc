Hourly & Private Chauffeur Service

By-the-Hour Booking · Flexible Itineraries · Bay Area Wide

Some days don't fit a single pickup-and-drop-off ride. Hourly service puts a private chauffeur and vehicle on your schedule for a stretch of time — waiting between stops, adjusting on the fly, and moving whenever you're ready.

What's Included

A dedicated chauffeur and vehicle for your full booking window

Waiting time between stops included, no rebooking

Flexible, adjustable itineraries — plans can change mid-ride

Ideal for shopping trips, meetings, or city days

Sedans, SUVs, and vans available depending on group size

Fixed, transparent hourly pricing — no surge

Your Schedule, Not a Route

Unlike a rideshare booked stop-to-stop, hourly service means your chauffeur is committed to your plans for the duration — not moving on to another fare the moment you step out. That's what makes it work for days that don't have a fixed shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum booking for hourly service?

Hourly bookings are available for a range of durations depending on your plans — contact our team with your itinerary and we'll recommend the right length.

Can the driver wait between stops?

Yes, that's the point of hourly service — your chauffeur and vehicle wait at each stop and are ready to go whenever you are, with no need to rebook a ride.

Can I change my itinerary during the booking?

Yes. Hourly service is built for flexible, spontaneous plans — just tell your chauffeur where to go next.
