Frequently Asked Questions

Which industries does Flying Limo serve?

Private Aviation, Executive Travel, Meetings & Events, Roadshows, and Travel Agencies, among others.

What makes Flying Limo a suitable partner for Private Aviation clients?

We coordinate closely with FBOs and flight schedules so ground transportation is ready the moment wheels touch down, anywhere in the world.

How does Flying Limo support Executive Travel programs?

Dedicated account management, consistent chauffeurs, and predictable, on-time pickups built around a demanding travel calendar.

Coordinated multi-vehicle logistics for attendees, speakers, and VIPs, managed from a single point of contact.

Consistent service standards and reservation management across every city on the itinerary, so the schedule holds no matter where it goes.

Why should a Travel Management Company partner with Flying Limo?

A single, reliable point of contact for worldwide ground transportation, backed by a personally vetted chauffeur network and one of the lowest error rates in the industry.
